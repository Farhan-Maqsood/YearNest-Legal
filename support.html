FAQ

Do my photos leave my iPhone?

YearNest builds cards on your device. Photos are not uploaded to a YearNest server. A card leaves the device only when you Share or Save to Photos.

Why does YearNest need Full Photo Access?

Full Access lets YearNest see photos across the year or month you choose. Limited Access is not enough to build a complete year card. Open iOS Settings → YearNest → Photos and choose Full Access.

How many free saves do I get?

Without YearNest+, you can save one card to Photos for free. Additional saves require YearNest+. Sharing may still be available.

What is YearNest+?

YearNest+ is an optional one-time purchase. It may unlock unlimited saves, remove the YearNest mark, and open earlier years. See the unlock screen in the app for current benefits.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in YearNest and tap Restore Purchase. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

What happens if I delete the app?

Cards and settings stored in YearNest History are local. Removing the app may delete them unless you saved cards to Photos or elsewhere. Purchased unlocks can usually be restored with Restore Purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or use Apple’s purchase support. PureNest cannot process App Store refunds directly.

How can I report a bug?

Email purenesttech@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, YearNest version, and a short description of the issue.
